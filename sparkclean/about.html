Our Story

From a Small Startup to a Trusted Cleaning Company

SparkClean Services was founded in 2014 with a simple mission: to provide reliable, high-quality cleaning services that people can truly depend on. What started as a two-person operation has grown into a team of over 50 dedicated cleaning professionals.

Our founder, Sarah Johnson, noticed a gap in the market for cleaning services that combined professional quality with genuine care for customers. She built SparkClean on the principles of trust, reliability, and attention to detail.

Today, we serve thousands of residential and commercial clients across Port Harcourt and surrounding areas, maintaining the same commitment to excellence that defined our very first cleaning job.
